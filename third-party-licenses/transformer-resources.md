# Transformer Explainer: observed build, model and external resources

Audit of the pinned source and installed build, 2026-10-27. This is an inventory, not a complete redistribution clearance. The parent repository ignores upstream checkouts and builds; their inclusion in a deployment must be assessed separately.

## Model files

- The pinned source `src/routes/+page.svelte` loads `static/model-v2/gpt2.onnx.part0` through `part62`. The checkout's 63 files concatenate, in numerical order, to 656,662,664 bytes with SHA-256 `ccf64e934094054603c5b7bd98191f0fd2ea0315a81e9e0d8104070f64413119`. This fingerprint identifies the observed files, not their legal status or origin.
- The [publication fingerprint inventory](publication-asset-fingerprints.json) includes each pinned chunk and the exact concatenation in numeric order; `python scripts/audit_publication.py <publication_directory>` can flag byte-identical renamed copies. Other reassemblies and altered files are outside this check, and a match or lack of matches does not determine redistribution rights.
- The [export script](https://github.com/poloclub/transformer-explainer/blob/bfe50afba10b9b560b84143ee1107d977defa74f/src/utils/model/export_to_onnx.py) calls `GPT.from_pretrained('gpt2')`; the [implementation](https://github.com/poloclub/transformer-explainer/blob/bfe50afba10b9b560b84143ee1107d977defa74f/src/utils/model/model.py) loads pretrained GPT-2 weights through Hugging Face. The authors' [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04619) also describes a nanoGPT GPT-2 small model converted to ONNX. These descriptions do not independently prove that the exact checked-in binary was built by that script or resolve its redistribution terms.
- The [openai-community/gpt2 card](https://huggingface.co/openai-community/gpt2) declares MIT. [OpenAI's GPT-2 repository license](openai-gpt2.LICENSE) is headed Modified MIT License and has additional wording. Record and evaluate both sources rather than treating either as a verified license for the exact ONNX file.

## Tokenizer downloaded at runtime

The patched app calls `AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained('Xenova/gpt2', { revision: 'bf2c7f02e0b826c60d03af341171bde20893da66' })`. The installed `@xenova/transformers` 2.17.2 tokenizer loader first checks a local model path and, when absent there, requests `tokenizer.json` and `tokenizer_config.json` from that commit, separately from the model chunks. A local copy takes precedence and is not revision-pinned by this option. The [Xenova/gpt2 card](https://huggingface.co/Xenova/gpt2) describes it as GPT-2 with ONNX weights but does not itself declare a license.

On 2026-10-27 the following pinned revisions and file SHA-256 hashes were observed:

| Repository and revision | File | SHA-256 |
| --- | --- | --- |

Parsing the two `tokenizer.json` files and comparing their JSON objects shows the `model.vocab` and `model.merges` values match exactly. Other fields, including preprocessing and added-token fields, differ, and the files' hashes differ. This comparison does not prove equivalent tokenizer behavior or establish the license of Xenova's files. Pinning the runtime revision identifies the requested snapshot but does not itself establish redistribution rights.

## Article images / 설명 이미지

The original `static/article_assets/embedding.png`, `QKV.png`, `attention.png`, `mlp.png` and `softmax.png` are screenshots whose separate provenance and redistribution rights have not been established. The patched article replaces these five images with original bilingual numerical examples; no requests for them were observed in the tested Korean and English article. The original files remain in the checkout and static build, so exclude them from a publication directory unless their rights are confirmed. Their pinned bytes are included in the selected publication fingerprint audit, which can also find unchanged renamed copies. Other assets require separate review; these replacements are not a general rights clearance.

## JavaScript and WebAssembly

- The [JS source-map notice inventory](transformer-js-source-map-notices.txt) lists 32 installed packages identified by an audit build. It is produced by `npx vite build --sourcemap` from `upstream/transformer-explainer`, then `python scripts/transformer_bundle_licenses.py` from the project root. `--check` compares the saved notice inventory with the present audit build. The maps refer to original `.svelte-kit/output/client` locations even though adapter-static copies them into `build/`. The audit maps must be regenerated after a normal build, and they do not independently prove bundle integrity.
- GSAP 3.13.0 is one identified package. Its installed manifest and source header refer to the [GSAP Standard No Charge License](https://gsap.com/standard-license/), **not MIT**. That license contains restrictions and requires preserving proprietary notices; review its current terms for the intended use. The inventory retains its source header and a link to the full terms.
- The source maps identify `onnxruntime-web` 1.23.0 and 1.14.0, and `onnxruntime-common` 1.14.0. Their installed npm packages declare MIT but do not contain a top-level license file. The retained [ONNX Runtime license](onnxruntime.LICENSE) was fetched from the upstream repository and is byte-identical at tags [v1.14.0](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/v1.14.0/LICENSE) and [v1.23.0](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/v1.23.0/LICENSE). The upstream [1.14.0 third-party notices](onnxruntime-1.14.0.ThirdPartyNotices.txt) and [1.23.0 third-party notices](onnxruntime-1.23.0.ThirdPartyNotices.txt) are also retained, without claiming they prove all build-specific transitive notices are covered.
- The built `ort-wasm-simd-threaded.jsep` asset was 23,696,346 bytes, SHA-256 `938bd5e845aaac341253f2e72af81c41be45e05c61ac055eedddee9010c57415`, byte-identical to `onnxruntime-web` 1.23.0's installed `dist/ort-wasm-simd-threaded.jsep.wasm`. Its compiled native dependencies are not individually audited here. The patched app no longer overrides the runtime path with jsDelivr; its Vite build includes a matching local WASM asset. In the tested Chrome configuration, real model generation succeeded at the server root and a local project prefix with the old CDN blocked and a successful local WASM response. Other browsers and runtime configurations can choose different assets and are not covered by that observation.
- The maps include ONNX Runtime's already-minified JS as a single source. The 1.14.0 package's own map exposes `@protobufjs/*`, `flatbuffers`, `guid-typescript`, `long`, `onnx-proto`, `protobufjs` and `worker-loader`. The [nested notice inventory](transformer-onnx-nested-notices.txt) compares each included source file with an installed package and retains the matching packages' shipped license texts. Worker-loader's inline runtime matches npm's 3.0.8 file; its [license](worker-loader-3.0.8.LICENSE) was taken from that package. Run `python scripts/transformer_onnx_nested_licenses.py --check` to repeat the local comparisons. Equal files are evidence of compatible versions, not proof of the exact versions used to build the published bundle.
- The audit-build source map embeds the installed ONNX Runtime Web 1.23.0 `ort.bundle.min.mjs` exactly. That package's own map lists six Google-copyrighted files derived from TensorFlow.js, each with an Apache 2.0 header. The [1.23 JavaScript nested notices](transformer-onnx-1.23-js-notices.txt) preserve the source headers, provenance comments and Apache license. The audit is reproducible with `python scripts/transformer_onnx_123_notices.py --check` after an audit build. Source-map membership does not establish which individual functions survive tree-shaking; this does not inspect WASM. The corresponding upstream source files are in the [ONNX Runtime v1.23.0 third-party directory](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/tree/v1.23.0/js/web/lib/wasm/jsep/webgpu/ops/3rd-party).
- `guid-typescript` 1.0.9 declares ISC and author `nicolas` in its npm metadata, but neither its installed package nor the observed [repository](https://github.com/snico-dev/guid-typescript) supplies a license text or copyright notice. Independent notices identify Nicolas Silva dos Santos ([Enreach, explicitly version 1.0.9](https://confloss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OSS/pages/852921/guid-typescript)); an [ObserveIT notice, page 4](https://files.observeit.com/docs/Observeit%20Open%20Source%20Notices%207.12.x.pdf) also lists Jose Romaniello. A [Canon notice, page 5](https://canon.a.bigcontent.io/v1/static/crs---third-party-eula---2025_1a1ae0a8ffb54988935a2bb48c00fe27) gives a different year, 2025, without specifying the package version. These are third-party claims, not the package's own notice. We have not verified the complete rightsholder list, year, or exact original notice for the bundled code; confirmation is still needed. The comparison also does not establish which native libraries are compiled into WASM, or cover CSS and runtime-fetched scripts.

## Other external resources

The HTML references the Jersey 10 font from Google Fonts. The math component imports CSS and font files from the same locked KaTeX 0.16.22 package as its JavaScript. The [KaTeX software MIT notice](katex-0.16.22.LICENSE) and the [SIL Open Font License 1.1 and font copyright and reserved-name notices](katex-fonts-OFL-1.1.txt), taken from the distributed font metadata, are retained separately. The font license restricts use of reserved names for modified fonts. Images and other static assets are not comprehensively audited.
