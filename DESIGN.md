# Model Atlas interface

Reference: [Meta DESIGN.md, VoltAgent](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/blob/main/design-md/meta/DESIGN.md), reviewed 2026-10-28. This is an independent visual analysis, not an official Meta specification.

Use its white canvas, cobalt `#0064e0`, deep ink `#0a1317`, soft gray `#f1f4f7`, pill actions and restrained rounded panels. Keep a clear heading/body/caption hierarchy, generous spacing and thin dividers. Use the existing sans-serif stack; do not copy Meta's proprietary font or branding. Learning content and model diagrams replace the reference's retail imagery.

The formula reader puts the current explanation on the left and the operation, carried values, substitution, result and growing derivation on the right. Both follow one shared step. On narrow screens, explanation precedes calculation. Long equations scroll locally. Concept help opens on pointer hover, keyboard focus or touch; Escape and outside activation dismiss it. Preserve bilingual content, numerical behavior, full-window state and original-lab links.

Use a full-height sticky sidebar on desktop, with brand, language, chapter navigation and three formula-start links. Do not add a top bar. On small screens, show a compact navigation grid before the lesson. Formula-start links restart the selected model at its first operation and preserve adjusted values within the current page. The standalone reader also preserves current model values on restart. Lessons proceed from guide and concepts through symbols, architecture and formulas, then calculation exercises, the lazy original lab and review. Contents and guided routes follow that order; external labs remain separate illustrative models.

Keep the Model Atlas wordmark without its former square icon. At 1180px and above, use a third column for the active model's connected calculations. Keep its model heading outside the independently scrollable list and contain wheel scrolling at both boundaries. Move the existing CNN/RNN/Transformer history instead of copying its IDs. Diffusion/GAN groups reflect their separate live exercises without implying shared weights or sequentially connected states. On narrower screens, place a bounded history viewport before the lesson. Preserve keyboard jumps, current inputs and original article links. Escape must keep concept help closed when hiding the card exposes another operator beneath a stationary pointer.
